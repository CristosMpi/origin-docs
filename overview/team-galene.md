# Team Galene

**Team Galene** is the student robotics and engineering team behind Project ORIGIN.

The team is based at the **4th General Lyceum of Ioannina (4ο ΓΕΛ Ιωαννίνων), Greece** and develops projects that combine robotics, electronics, software, artificial intelligence, mechanical design, and real-world problem solving.

ORIGIN is one of Team Galene's most ambitious projects because it brings those disciplines together around a single objective: applying engineering to the protection of cultural heritage.

## ORIGIN development team

The current ORIGIN project team consists of **Christos Mpirmpos** and **Evelina Tsiagkaveli**.

The team is coached by **Anastasia Giannakou**, Director of the 4th General Lyceum of Ioannina – Akademia.

Rather than separating the project into isolated school subjects, Team Galene works across the complete engineering process: research, requirements, prototyping, electronics, CAD, fabrication, programming, testing, documentation, presentation, partnerships, and deployment planning.

## How the team develops ORIGIN

ORIGIN is developed using an iterative engineering workflow.

```text
Problem research
      ↓
Requirements
      ↓
Concept development
      ↓
Prototype
      ↓
Testing and validation
      ↓
Revision
      ↓
Field deployment
      ↓
Feedback and improvement
```

This approach means that a failed test or manufacturing problem is treated as engineering information rather than simply as a setback. Designs are revised as the team learns more about the hardware, software, fabrication process, and real deployment conditions.

## Multidisciplinary development

ORIGIN requires the team to work across several technical areas at the same time.

### Electronics

Team Galene develops the Rosetta electronics platform, works with sensors and power systems, prepares PCB designs for manufacturing, evaluates components, and integrates electronics into the physical product.

### Mechanical design

The team designs enclosures, mounts, module interfaces, sensor placement, fabrication methods, and the physical architecture needed for field use.

### Software and AI

ORIGIN includes embedded firmware, communications, data handling, and the Centaurus AI layer. Software development is connected directly to the capabilities and limitations of the physical system.

### Testing

Testing is performed at component, subsystem, and full-system levels. The project includes a dedicated testing and validation section so that results, procedures, and limitations can be documented rather than relying only on demonstrations.

### Deployment

Team Galene also works on the non-laboratory parts of engineering: installation, maintenance, transportation, partnerships, field constraints, and how ORIGIN can be used by people outside the development team.

## Competition and international development

Team Galene develops ORIGIN within a broader robotics and STEM competition environment. In 2026, the team qualified to represent Greece at the **World Robot Olympiad International Final in Puerto Rico** in the Future Innovators category.

Competition provides an important development deadline and a way to communicate the project, but ORIGIN is intended to extend beyond the competition itself. The documentation, custom electronics, deployment work, partnerships, and pilot activities are designed to give the project continuity after an event is over.

## Real-world collaboration

A central goal of ORIGIN is to connect student engineering with real heritage environments.

The project has included collaboration and deployment planning connected with the **Archaeological Museum of Durrës, Albania**, where ORIGIN units are intended to support a pilot implementation.

Team Galene has also built relationships with technology companies, sponsors, organizations, and other partners that support different parts of the engineering process, from PCB fabrication and manufacturing to prototyping equipment and materials.

These relationships are documented separately under [Partners & Sponsors](../partners-sponsors/README.md) and [Deployments](../deployments/README.md).

## Open engineering and documentation

Team Galene treats documentation as part of the engineering work.

ORIGIN Docs are intended to record not only the final result but also architecture decisions; hardware revisions; testing procedures; known limitations; manufacturing information; maintenance processes; deployment lessons; and future development.

This makes the project easier to maintain inside the team and more useful to students, engineers, educators, partners, and institutions that want to understand how ORIGIN works.

## Project values

The development of ORIGIN is guided by a few practical principles:

**Build for a real problem.** Technology is used because it can improve the solution, not simply because it is available.

**Test what we claim.** Features should be supported by prototypes, measurements, or clearly identified development status.

**Document limitations.** A technical project becomes more credible when its boundaries are visible.

**Design for iteration.** ORIGIN is expected to change as the team learns from manufacturing and deployment.

**Keep people in the loop.** Monitoring and AI should support heritage professionals and operators rather than replace human responsibility.
