# Partners & Sponsors

Project ORIGIN is developed by Team Galene with support from institutions, deployment collaborators, technology companies, manufacturing providers, and organizations that contribute to different parts of the engineering process.

This section records those relationships in a way that is useful to readers without confusing sponsorship, collaboration, deployment planning, technical validation, or product endorsement.

## Why this section exists

ORIGIN combines electronics, PCB manufacturing, mechanical prototyping, additive manufacturing, laser fabrication, 3D scanning, software, field testing, and cultural-heritage deployment planning. No single student team can reproduce every industrial capability internally.

External support therefore helps Team Galene move from design files and early prototypes toward hardware that can be manufactured, assembled, tested, documented, and deployed.

At the same time, public documentation must describe those relationships accurately.

A sponsor may provide equipment, materials, fabrication support, engineering tools, manufacturing credit, or access to a technical workflow. That support does **not** automatically mean that the sponsor has designed ORIGIN, certified it, validated its performance, or endorsed every technical claim in these docs.

Similarly, a deployment partner may collaborate on planning or evaluation without being a commercial sponsor.

## Relationship categories

ORIGIN documentation separates external relationships into three practical categories.

### Project and deployment partners

Partners are organizations or institutions that collaborate with Team Galene around the project itself, deployment planning, evaluation, education, or access to a real operating environment.

The principal heritage deployment collaboration documented for 2026 is the planned pilot with the **Archaeological Museum of Durrës, Albania**.

See [Partners](partners.md).

### Official sponsors

Sponsors provide material, equipment, manufacturing, design, or technical support that contributes to ORIGIN development.

The current official sponsor lineup is **Xometry**; **AISLER**; **eSUN**; **SCULPFUN**; **EasyEDA**; **JLCPCB**; and **Matter and Form**.

This list follows Team Galene's current official sponsor communication for ORIGIN.

See [Sponsors](sponsors.md).

### Acknowledged contributors

Some people, institutions, technical communities, educators, and organizations may contribute advice, facilities, review, access, logistics, or other forms of support without fitting neatly into either of the categories above.

Those contributions are recorded under [Acknowledgements](acknowledgements.md) when appropriate.

## Sponsor support and engineering independence

External support is important to ORIGIN, but engineering responsibility remains with Team Galene.

Team Galene is responsible for system requirements; architecture decisions; PCB design decisions; mechanical design; firmware and software behavior; integration; testing; validation; deployment decisions; documentation; and technical claims made publicly about ORIGIN.

A manufacturing sponsor producing a board, for example, does not become responsible for the electrical design of that board. A materials sponsor does not certify the final enclosure. A tool sponsor does not validate the result produced with that tool.

This distinction is especially important in a public engineering project because supplier capability and project validation are different things.

## Transparency rules

Team Galene should apply the following rules whenever a partner or sponsor is mentioned publicly.

### Describe only confirmed relationships

An organization should not be described as a sponsor or partner until the relationship has actually been agreed or publicly confirmed.

### Do not publish confidential commercial details

Public documentation should not expose private voucher or coupon codes; account identifiers; private contact information; unpublished contract terms; private shipping details; confidential pricing; and internal sponsor correspondence.

Where a sponsor provides financial or manufacturing credit, the public docs may describe the type of support without exposing credentials or codes.

### Do not imply certification

Phrases such as "certified by", "approved by", or "validated by" should only be used when a real formal certification or approval exists.

Normal sponsorship is not certification.

### Keep the list current

Sponsors and partnerships change over time. If a relationship ends, changes scope, or moves from planned to active, this section should be updated rather than preserving an outdated status indefinitely.

### Separate project claims from sponsor claims

Specifications and validation results in ORIGIN Docs should be supported by Team Galene's own evidence or by clearly cited manufacturer specifications. They should not be presented as true merely because a sponsor supplied the relevant component, service, or tool.

## Relationship with the Durrës pilot

The Durrës pilot is documented as a **planned deployment program**, not as a completed operational installation.

The Archaeological Museum of Durrës is therefore described as a deployment collaborator connected with the planned five-unit pilot. Public wording should continue to reflect the actual commissioning status.

See [Durrës Pilot](../deployments/durres-pilot.md).

## Logos and trademarks

Company names, product names, and logos remain the property of their respective owners.

Displaying a sponsor logo in Team Galene material communicates the sponsorship relationship; it does not transfer ownership of the mark and should not be interpreted as a broader corporate endorsement beyond the documented relationship.

Team Galene should use supplied or officially published brand assets according to each organization's applicable brand guidance.

## Documentation policy

Whenever this section is updated, maintainers should check that:

1. the organization is named correctly;
2. its role is described accurately;
3. no confidential information is included;
4. sponsorship is not confused with certification;
5. planned deployment relationships are not written as completed deployments;
6. the sponsor list matches Team Galene's current official communication;
7. discontinued relationships are archived or reclassified rather than silently misrepresented.
