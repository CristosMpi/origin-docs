# Acknowledgements

Project ORIGIN is developed by Team Galene, but the project also benefits from the institutions, collaborators, sponsors, educators, and technical communities that make ambitious student engineering possible.

This page records those contributions without confusing acknowledgement with authorship, certification, or ownership of the project.

## Team Galene

ORIGIN is developed by **Team Galene** of the **4th General Lyceum of Ioannina (4ο ΓΕΛ Ιωαννίνων), Greece**.

The current ORIGIN development team is **Christos Mpirmpos** and **Evelina Tsiagkaveli**.

The team is coached by **Anastasia Giannakou**, Director of the 4th General Lyceum of Ioannina – Akademia.

The project combines electronics, mechanical design, software, AI, fabrication, testing, documentation, sponsorship coordination, and deployment planning into one multidisciplinary engineering effort.

## 4th General Lyceum of Ioannina

Team Galene acknowledges the **4th General Lyceum of Ioannina** as the institutional home of the project.

The school environment makes it possible for the team to develop ORIGIN as more than a single competition prototype and to continue work across research, manufacturing, testing, documentation, and international participation.

## Archaeological Museum of Durrës

Team Galene acknowledges the **Archaeological Museum of Durrës, Albania** for the collaboration connected with the planned ORIGIN pilot.

The planned five-unit pilot is especially important because ORIGIN is intended for real cultural-heritage environments. Feedback from a genuine site can expose constraints that cannot be reproduced fully in laboratory testing.

The deployment remains documented as planned until installation and commissioning evidence confirms operational status.

## Why acknowledgements matter

A multidisciplinary project can easily blur the difference between the people who design the system and the organizations that enable the work.

ORIGIN therefore uses the following distinctions **Team Galene** — project developer and engineering owner; **institutional home** — the school environment supporting the team; **deployment partner** — organization collaborating around real-world use and evaluation; **sponsor** — organization providing equipment, materials, manufacturing, tools, or other project support; and **technical source** — manufacturer, standard, publication, or community source used to support a technical statement.

Keeping those categories separate makes the documentation more accurate and avoids overstating any organization's role.

## Credit and attribution policy

When third-party materials are used in ORIGIN Docs, maintainers should respect the applicable attribution and licensing requirements.

This includes logos; photographs; diagrams; datasheet excerpts; CAD files; code; libraries; documentation; research publications; and other copyrighted or licensed material.

A sponsorship relationship does not automatically grant unrestricted rights to reuse a company's visual assets or technical content.

## Sponsor graphics

The official sponsor graphic used by Team Galene should be treated as a visual summary of the current sponsor list.

When the lineup changes, the visual asset and the written sponsor documentation should be reviewed together so that the public record stays consistent.

## Keeping acknowledgements accurate

Acknowledgements should be updated when a new partner becomes active; a sponsor relationship is formally confirmed; a sponsor relationship ends; a deployment changes status; a contributor makes a significant documented contribution; a public attribution is required by a license; and a previous acknowledgement becomes inaccurate.

Historical contributions should not necessarily be erased when a relationship ends. Where useful, they can be moved into an archived or historical context so the project's development history remains understandable.

## Final note

ORIGIN is a student-led engineering project, but it exists inside a much larger network of educators, manufacturers, technology providers, institutions, and communities.

Team Galene's responsibility is to use that support well: document it accurately, protect confidential information, credit contributors appropriately, and remain accountable for the engineering decisions made in Project ORIGIN.
